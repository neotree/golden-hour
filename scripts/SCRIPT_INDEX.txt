All scripts are self-contained R files. They locate their own directory at
runtime, so they must be run from within the 02-CODE folder or using Rscript

    Rscript 02-CODE/<script_name>.R

Or from within 02-CODE/:
    Rscript <script_name>.R

All scripts are ASCII-only (DSH compliance: no Unicode or special characters).

02_outcome_analysis.R  [RETIRED AS PRIMARY ANALYSIS]
-----------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
  Original logistic regression analysis comparing DCC vs None, S2S vs None,
  and Any-GH vs None within the intervention period. Also includes period
  comparisons (intervention vs historical) for NND and normal temperature.

RETIREMENT NOTE:
  This script is retained for archival purposes but its primary analysis
  (DCC vs None logistic regression) is no longer reported as a main result.
  Reason: INDICATION BIAS. Healthier babies are more likely to (a) receive
  DCC/S2S (not requiring immediate resuscitation) and (b) survive. A naive
  comparison of DCC-received vs not-received therefore confounds intervention
  effect with selection into the intervention. The ITS framework (Scripts 04,
  04b) is the appropriate primary method for quality improvement evaluation.
  See script header and methodology change documentation for full rationale.

POPULATION:
  SMCH inborn. NND analyses restricted to direct-match records only
  (match_type == "direct_match").

KEY OUTPUTS:
  02a_delivinter_summary.csv
  02b_dcc_outcome_table.csv
  02c_s2s_outcome_table.csv
  02d_anygh_outcome_table.csv
  02e_period_effect_summary.csv
  02_outcome_analysis_log.txt

04_its_analysis.R
-----------------
PURPOSE:
  Interrupted Time Series (ITS) primary analysis of monthly normal admission
  temperature using segmented linear regression. This is the ORIGINAL analysis
  (January 2022 - October 2025, 46 months, 12 post-intervention months).

  Retained for archival / sensitivity comparison. The PRIMARY analysis as of
  June 2026 is Script 04b (January 2023 window, 17 post-intervention months).

ITS MODEL:
  pct_normal ~ time + intervention + time_after
  Weighted by monthly temperature recording count.
  Ljung-Box test for autocorrelation; Newey-West HAC SEs (lag=2) if p<0.05.

COEFFICIENTS:
  b1 = pre-intervention monthly trend (pp/month)
  b2 = immediate level change at November 2024 (pp)
  b3 = change in monthly slope post-intervention (pp/month)

KEY OUTPUTS:
  04a_its_coefficients.csv
  04b_its_monthly_fitted_primary.csv
  04c_its_monthly_fitted_sensitivity.csv
  04_its_analysis_log.txt

04b_its_analysis_excl2022.R  [CURRENT PRIMARY ANALYSIS]
--------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
  ITS analysis using the PRIMARY analysis window (January 2023 - March 2026,
  39 months: 22 pre + 17 post). This is the recommended analysis for reporting.

  Excludes 2022 because temperature recording rates in 2022 were anomalously
  low (~48-55% normal vs ~75-85% in 2023-2024). Power analysis (Script 05)
  shows including 2022 approximately halves power by inflating residual sigma.
  Script 04 (Jan 2022 window) is retained as the sensitivity check.

  Updated March 2026: extended from October 2025 to March 2026, adding 5
  additional post-intervention months. The previously-significant declining
  slope (b3 = -1.35 pp/month, p=0.012 in the May 2026 version) was found to
  be a seasonal truncation artefact: the old analysis ended at October 2025,
  which is historically the coldest month. Adding November 2025 - March 2026
  (summer recovery) eliminates the spurious b3 signal.

ITS MODEL: identical to Script 04. See above.

CURRENT RESULTS (June 2026, 17 post months):
  Primary (Jan 2023): b2=+2.84 pp (p=0.46), b3=-0.33 pp/mo (p=0.40) -- NS
  Sensitivity (Jan 2022): b2=-0.52 pp (p=0.92), b3=-0.63 pp/mo (p=0.17) -- NS

KEY OUTPUTS:
  04b_its_coefficients.csv
  04b_its_monthly_fitted.csv
  04b_its_plot.png
  04b_its_analysis_log.txt

04c_its_seasonality_correction.R  [SEASONAL SENSITIVITY: FOURIER ADJUSTMENT]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
  Seasonal-adjusted ITS analysis. Adds harmonic (Fourier) terms to the
  regression model to control for the annual temperature cycle explicitly,
  allowing b2 and b3 to estimate the intervention effect over and above
  expected seasonal variation.

  Addresses the question: "After accounting for the fact that October is
  always cold and January is always warm, is there still an intervention
  effect on admission temperature?"

SEASONAL MODEL:
  pct_normal ~ time + intervention + time_after + sin_t + cos_t
  where sin_t = sin(2 * pi * month_num / 12)
        cos_t = cos(2 * pi * month_num / 12)
  The sin/cos pair captures one full annual cycle using only 2 extra df.

COMPARISON:
  Runs both the unadjusted model (same as 04b) and the seasonally-adjusted
  model on the same data, producing a direct comparison of b2 and b3 estimates
  before and after seasonal adjustment. If results are similar, seasonal
  confounding is not materially affecting the unadjusted estimates.

ANALYSIS WINDOWS: same as 04b (primary Jan 2023, sensitivity Jan 2022).

INTERPRETATION:
  - Adjusted b3 ~ unadjusted b3, both NS: seasonal variation is not masking
    or creating a spurious effect. The null result in 04b is robust.
  - Adjusted b3 becomes significant (negative): intervention improves
    temperature but effect was hidden by seasonal noise in 04b.
  - Adjusted b3 becomes NS when 04b was significant: old result was driven
    by seasonal confounding (the situation in the May 2026 analysis).

KEY OUTPUTS:
  04c_its_seasonality_correction_log.txt
  04c_its_seasonality_correction_coefficients.csv
  04c_its_seasonality_correction_monthly.csv
  04c_its_seasonality_correction_plot.png    -- 2-panel: raw fit + adj data

04d_seasonal_comparison.R  [SEASONAL SENSITIVITY: SAME-SEASON YEAR-ON-YEAR]
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
  Non-parametric seasonal control: compares each intervention month against
  the same calendar month(s) in the historical period. Seasonal effects
  cancel by design (November 2025 is compared to November 2023 + Nov 2024,
  not to October or January of any year).

  Complements the Fourier-adjusted ITS (Script 04c) with a transparent,
  assumption-light approach that is easier to communicate to clinical
  audiences.

METHOD:
  1. For each intervention month (Nov 2024 - Mar 2026), compute the
     historical mean for the same calendar month across the primary
     historical window (Jan 2023 - Oct 2024).
  2. Compute: matched_diff = observed_int - historical_mean_same_month
  3. One-sample t-test on matched_diffs (H0: mean_diff = 0).
  4. Season-level analysis using Zimbabwe seasons:
       Warm/wet: November - April
       Cool/dry: May - October
  5. Sensitivity: repeat using Jan 2022 - Oct 2024 as historical window.

INTERPRETATION:
  A positive mean difference indicates intervention-period temperatures
  are higher than the historical same-month average. A non-significant
  result (after removing seasonal effects) suggests no detectable
  change attributable to the intervention.

KEY OUTPUTS:
  04d_seasonal_comparison_log.txt
  04d_seasonal_comparison_monthly.csv    -- month-matched table with diffs
  04d_seasonal_comparison_plot.png       -- 3-panel: series, calendar-month
                                            dot plot, difference-over-time

05_power_analysis.R
-------------------
PURPOSE:
  Simulation-based power analysis for the ITS segmented regression (Script 04).
  Estimates power to detect clinically meaningful b2 (level change) and b3
  (slope change) effects, across a range of candidate effect sizes.

  Uses observed pre-intervention residual sigma as the noise parameter.
  Includes AR(1) sensitivity run to bound the effect of autocorrelation on
  power.

KEY FINDING:
  Including 2022 data (anomalously low recording rates) inflates residual
  sigma from ~122 to ~196 pp, roughly halving power. This analysis motivated
  the Jan 2023 primary window in Script 04b.

KEY OUTPUTS:
  05a_power_b2.csv / 05b_power_b3.csv / 05c_power_summary.csv
  05_power_analysis_log.txt

07_power_by_preperiod.R
-----------------------
PURPOSE:
  Sweeps across all possible pre-intervention window lengths (4 to 34 months)
  and estimates ITS power at each length for both b2 and b3. Identifies the
  window length that maximises power.

  Motivation: Script 05 showed a counterintuitive result -- the shorter
  Jan 2023 window has higher power than the longer Jan 2022 window, because
  the anomalous 2022 data inflates residual sigma and overwhelms the benefit

08_maternal_script_uptake.R
---------------------------
PURPOSE:
  Compares DCC and S2S uptake across two parallel data streams over the
  17-month intervention period (November 2024 - March 2026):

  Part A -- NeoTree (NICU admissions):
    Monthly uptake from the delivinter multi-select field.
    Denominator: known delivinter (Y or N, excluding Unknown/U).
    Population: SMCH inborn non-readmission.
    Note: high Unknown rate (34% overall; up to 48% Jul-Oct 2025) reflects
    documentation gaps, not absence of intervention.

  Part B -- Maternal outcomes script (PRIMARY source):
    Monthly uptake from Prisca's delivery-log data (all deliveries).
    Covers ~4.7x more records than NeoTree (23,726 live births vs ~7,032
    NICU admissions). This is the primary source for population-level
    coverage reporting.
    DCC overall: 98.9%; S2S overall: 92.8% (of eligible with known outcome).

  Part C -- LBW subgroup (birthweight < 2500 g):
    DCC: 96.7%; S2S: 86.3% among eligible LBW with known outcome.

KEY OUTPUTS:
  08a_neotree_monthly_uptake.csv
  08b_maternal_monthly_uptake.csv
  08c_lbw_monthly_uptake.csv
  08d_uptake_comparison_summary.csv

12_mortality_its.R
------------------
PURPOSE:
  Interrupted Time Series analysis of monthly neonatal death (NND) rate
  among SMCH inborn babies. Outcome: NND rate = NND / (NND + DC) x 100.
  Excludes TRO, TRH, DAMA, ABS, and records without a definitive outcome
  (requires match_type == "direct_match").

  Runs three parallel ITS analyses:
    (1) All inborn               (primary)
    (2) LBW subgroup (BW <2500g)
    (3) NBW subgroup (BW >=2500g)

  Sensitivity: extends historical window to January 2022.

KEY FINDINGS (June 2026, 17 post months):
  All inborn: b2=+0.38 pp (p=0.82), b3=+0.17 pp/mo (p=0.26) -- NOT significant
  LBW:        b2=-5.25 pp (p=0.14), b3=+0.66 pp/mo (p=0.043*) -- slope sig.
  NBW:        b2=+1.71 pp (p=0.17), b3=-0.14 pp/mo (p=0.23) -- NOT significant

  The LBW slope (b3=+0.66, p=0.043) requires clinical team review before
  interpretation. Possible explanations: seasonal case-mix shift, sparse
  denominator in Dec 2025/Jan 2026, regression to mean, or genuine signal.

KEY OUTPUTS:
  12_mortality_its_log.txt
  12a_mortality_monthly.csv
  12b_its_coefficients.csv
  12c_mortality_its_plot.png    -- all-inborn ITS
  12d_lbw_mortality_its_plot.png -- LBW ITS

12b_mortality_its_fourier.R  [MORTALITY SEASONALITY SENSITIVITY]
-----------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
  Fourier-adjusted ITS analysis of monthly neonatal death (NND) rate.
  Analogue of Script 04c for the mortality outcome. Adds harmonic (Fourier)
  seasonal terms to the mortality ITS model to test whether the LBW slope
  signal (b3=+0.66, p=0.043 in Script 12) persists after seasonal adjustment.

  Addresses the question: "Is the LBW rising slope driven by seasonal
  case-mix variation, or is it a genuine post-intervention trend?"

SEASONAL MODEL:
  pct_nnd ~ time + intervention + time_after + sin_t + cos_t
  Weighted OLS. Newey-West HAC SEs (lag=2) if Ljung-Box p < 0.05.

ANALYSES:
  (1) All inborn -- primary (Jan 2023 - Mar 2026)
  (2) LBW subgroup (BW <2500g) -- primary
  (3) NBW subgroup (BW >=2500g) -- primary
  (4) All inborn -- sensitivity (Jan 2022 - Mar 2026)

KEY FINDINGS (June 2026):
  All inborn:
    Unadjusted b2=+0.38 pp (p=0.82), b3=+0.17 pp/mo (p=0.26) -- NS
    Adjusted   b2=+0.16 pp (p=0.93), b3=+0.17 pp/mo (p=0.29) -- NS
    sin/cos: both NS (no residual seasonal pattern)

  LBW:
    Unadjusted b2=-5.25 pp (p=0.14), b3=+0.66 pp/mo (p=0.043*)
    Adjusted   b2=-6.00 pp (p=0.10), b3=+0.61 pp/mo (p=0.065 -- NS)
    The LBW slope is attenuated and becomes NS after seasonal adjustment.
    Suggests seasonal/case-mix variation contributes to the unadjusted signal.

  NBW:
    Unadjusted b2=+1.71 pp (p=0.17), b3=-0.14 pp/mo (p=0.23) -- NS
    Adjusted   b2=+1.61 pp (p=0.22), b3=-0.13 pp/mo (p=0.27) -- NS

DEPENDENCY: Script 12 should run first for context, but 12b is self-contained.

KEY OUTPUTS:
  12b_mortality_fourier_log.txt
  12b_mortality_fourier_monthly.csv
  12b_mortality_fourier_coefficients.csv
  12b_mortality_fourier_plot_all.png    -- 2-panel: raw fit + adjusted rates
  12b_mortality_fourier_plot_lbw.png    -- LBW subgroup (same format)
